## Context

Ecosystem is an app for hotel guests. It brings room services, such as room service, housekeeping or the wake-up call, into one place together with the city businesses the concierge recommends.

## Challenge

During a stay, guests have to call the front desk, search online or ask in the lobby to sort out things as simple as ordering food or finding a barbershop.

The app had to replace those steps without becoming yet another heavy app nobody wants to download for a couple of nights.

## Approach

The home screen starts from a question: "What are you looking for today?". Below it, it brings together four entry points:

- Access to the room.
- Categories to explore.
- "Pick up where you left off", to resume an order or a booking.
- The concierge's recommendations.

For guests who would rather type or talk, I added a conversational assistant with quick suggestions such as "What's on today?" or "Order Italian food".

I chose a dark interface with a gold accent, in keeping with a hotel experience and comfortable to use at night.

## Solution

Each business has a profile with its photo, verification, rating, a short description, its opening hours with an "Open now" status, and its services and products split into tabs, so booking or ordering takes only a few taps.

The assistant welcomes the guest by name and handles the same task as search, but in natural language.

## Learnings

In an app people use for a short time, every screen has to deliver value the first time it's used.

Combining hotel services with city businesses made the app more useful than a digital room-service menu.
