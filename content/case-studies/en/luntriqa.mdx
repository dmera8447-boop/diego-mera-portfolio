## Context

Luntriqa builds tailored technology and AI for insurers and brokers across Latin America.

Its offer is technical, so the site had to explain it quickly to decision-makers who aren't necessarily technical.

## Challenge

The site had to make clear within seconds who Luntriqa is for and what it solves, and lead visitors into a sales conversation.

At the same time, it had to build trust in a conservative industry without falling into generic "digital transformation" language.

## Approach

The value proposition sits in the headline, with no detours: tailored technology and AI for insurers and brokers in Latin America. The brand colour highlights only what sets the company apart.

Next to the headline, a sample conversation with the assistant shows the product at work instead of describing it.

I kept one primary action to talk to the team and a secondary one for visitors who still want to explore.

## Solution

The site uses a light palette with a brand violet, comfortable reading type and short navigation.

The hero brings together the proposition, both actions and the assistant view, so the first screen already tells the whole story.

The site shares its visual language with Luntriqa's claims platform, so moving from marketing to product feels continuous.

## Learnings

In B2B, showing the product at work is more convincing than any adjective.

Designing the site and the product on the same visual system also saved work and made the brand feel more solid.
