## Context

Luntriqa is Shepwashi's insurtech: technology and artificial intelligence for insurers across Latin America and the Caribbean.

The site speaks to insurers, from leadership and the C-suite to executive teams. It doesn't speak to end customers, because the company is the one that evaluates and buys.

## Challenge

The previous site wasn't built to convert prospects:

- Mr. I, Luntriqa's executive artificial intelligence, wasn't well positioned.
- The home page was missing key sections.
- The platforms had no page of their own to explain themselves.

## Approach

I redesigned the home page and defined the page architecture together with part of the development team: home, platforms, Mr. I, About and navigation.

The whole journey points to one action: requesting a demo. The main button is on every page, and the form arrives with the platform or interest already selected, so visitors don't have to repeat what their click already said. The secondary action is getting to know Mr. I.

## Solution

- **Redesigned home page:** the value proposition goes straight into the headline, and next to it a conversation with the assistant shows the product in action instead of describing it.
- **Mr. I page:** gives the executive AI the place it lacked on the previous site.
- **Platform pages:** each one with its own space to explain itself, plus a dropdown menu to reach any of them from any page.
- **About:** who is behind Luntriqa, to build trust in a conservative industry.

I worked on a system of components and tokens, so every page shares the same pattern. Shepwashi's in-house team built the site, and it's live.

## Learnings

In B2B, showing the product at work convinces more than any adjective.

When a site has a single main action, every new page is designed around one simple question: does this bring the visitor closer to the demo?

Designing the site and the product with the same visual system saved work and made the brand feel more solid.
